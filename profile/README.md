## Research focus

- Shallow geothermal energy under climate change (CMIP6, SSP scenarios, finite-line-source BHE theory)
- Geospatial data science: Google Earth Engine, QGIS, rasterio, geopandas, folium / Leaflet
- Uncertainty quantification across climate-model ensembles
- Making research explorable: interactive maps, feasibility tools, grounded AI assistants
